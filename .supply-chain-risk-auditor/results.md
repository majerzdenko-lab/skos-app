## Executive Summary

The project uses a modern, well-chosen dependency stack. The majority of dependencies are backed by large organisations (Prisma, Auth0/Okta, Socket.IO, Remix/Shopify, Meta, Vercel/Vite). Four dependencies carry at least one risk factor:

- **`@react-pdf/renderer`** is the highest-risk dependency: single individual maintainer with 410 open issues and no dedicated security contact.
- **`bcrypt`** uses a native C extension (FFI), which expands the attack surface; the backing org (`kelektiv`) has only one public member.
- **`jsonwebtoken`** had four medium-severity CVEs in 2022 (all fixed in v9); as the JWT authentication library it is security-critical.
- **`resend`** (email delivery SDK) has low star count (~913) relative to the rest of the stack.

## Suggested Alternatives

**`jsonwebtoken`** (1 risk factor — past CVEs): The four 2022 CVEs are fixed in v9 (the version in use). Consider migrating to **`jose`** (`panva/jose`, ~6k stars, actively maintained by Filip Skokan, a known W3C/IETF contributor) which has no published CVEs, supports modern JOSE standards, and has a cleaner API. This is a moderate migration effort.

**`resend`** (1 risk factor — low popularity): The SDK is young (~913 stars) but the underlying service (Resend) is well-funded and the SDK is actively maintained. If vendor-neutrality is a concern, **`nodemailer`** (~17k stars, 15+ years of history) with any SMTP provider is the established alternative.
